Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

BeginVideo AI is a desktop application that helps creators prepare and upload user-selected video content to TikTok as drafts.

Information we process

The application may process video files selected by the user, TikTok OAuth authorization data, access tokens, refresh tokens, upload status information, and basic account identifiers returned by TikTok APIs.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the user's TikTok account, prepare user-selected videos, upload content requested by the user, and check upload status.

Storage

Application configuration and authorization data are stored locally on the user's device. BeginVideo AI does not request or store the user's TikTok password.

Sharing

Data is shared with TikTok only when necessary to provide requested TikTok authentication and content upload functions. We do not sell personal information.

User control

Users choose which videos are uploaded and may stop using the application at any time. Users may also revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of the BeginVideo AI application.
