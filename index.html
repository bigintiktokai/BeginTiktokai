BeginVideo AI

Prepare videos and upload drafts to TikTok

BeginVideo AI is a desktop application for creators to prepare videos for TikTok. It can convert user-selected videos to vertical 9:16 format, split long videos into manageable episodes, organize upload-ready files, and upload user-selected videos to TikTok as drafts through authorized TikTok APIs.

Users choose the content they want to process and explicitly start each upload. After a draft is uploaded, TikTok notifies the authorized user so they can continue editing and complete the post in TikTok.

BeginVideo AI uses TikTok Login Kit for authorization and does not request or store TikTok passwords.
