BeginTiktok

Video preparation and TikTok upload tool

BeginTiktok is a desktop utility that helps users prepare their own videos for TikTok by converting them to vertical 9:16 format, splitting long videos into episodes, organizing upload-ready files, and sending user-selected content to TikTok through authorized TikTok APIs.

The user remains in control of which videos are processed and uploaded. BeginTiktok does not request or store TikTok passwords.
